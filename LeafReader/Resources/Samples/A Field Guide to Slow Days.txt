A Field Guide to Slow Days

Leaf Editions

1. A slower morning

Let the morning begin before you ask anything of it. Open the window. Put water on for tea. Read a few pages while the city gathers itself.

2. Make a small journey

Walk somewhere without a destination. Take the path beside the trees. Stop when you find a bench with a view of something alive.

3. Leave a little space

A day does not need to be full to be complete. Leave space for a conversation that runs long, a meal made slowly, a book you are happy to return to.

4. A quiet ending

At dusk, put your unfinished work aside. Notice what the day gave you. Tomorrow can wait until tomorrow.
